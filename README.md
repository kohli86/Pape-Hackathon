# AquaGrid — Restore the Flow

**AquaGrid by Vector Coders** is a beginner-friendly 2D browser puzzle game inspired by **UN Sustainable Development Goal 6: Clean Water and Sanitation**.

Players act as water-systems engineers. Rotate pipe sections to reconnect a reservoir to a school, restoring the clean-water supply while learning why reliable water infrastructure matters.

## Play the game

No installation, package manager, or build step is required.

1. Open the published GitHub Pages URL, or download the repository.
2. To run locally, open `index.html` in a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari).
3. Click pipe sections to rotate them clockwise by 90 degrees.
4. Press **TEST WATER FLOW** to check whether water reaches the school.
5. Use **HINT** for a clue, **RESET** to restart the current puzzle, or **NEXT LEVEL** after completing a mission.

## Endless procedural levels

AquaGrid no longer uses a list of hand-written levels. Each new level is generated by the game itself.

1. The generator creates a grid and places the reservoir and school at opposite corners.
2. It builds a route from the cell beside the reservoir to the cell beside the school by randomly interleaving north and east moves. This creates a valid beginner-friendly route.
3. As levels rise, the generator adds small detours. Each detour replaces one pipe-to-pipe step with a three-step bend around unused cells, making the route longer without breaking it.
4. It turns each route cell into a straight or elbow pipe based on the directions to its previous and next cells.
5. It scrambles the solution pipes, guarantees the first pipe is disconnected from the reservoir at the start, and adds optional decoy pipes away from the solution route.
6. The water-flow checker uses **breadth-first search (BFS)** to follow matching pipe openings from the reservoir. The level is complete only when this search reaches the school.

The level counter has no three-level cap. Grid size increases from 6 × 6 to 10 × 10 as the player progresses. After that, randomized routes, detours, pipe rotations, and decoys continue to create new challenges. Random layouts can theoretically repeat, but the generator can keep creating levels without a fixed endpoint.

## Controls

- **Click a pipe:** rotate it 90° clockwise.
- **Test Water Flow:** check whether water can travel from the reservoir to the school.
- **Hint:** highlight the next solution pipe that is not correctly aligned.
- **Reset:** restore the current level's original pipe arrangement.
- **Next Level:** generate a new randomized puzzle after completing the current level.

## Difficulty progression

- Levels 1–3: 6 × 6 grid, short routes and simple pipe rotations.
- Levels 4–6: 7 × 7 grid, with more route detours.
- Levels 7–9: 8 × 8 grid.
- Levels 10–12: 9 × 9 grid.
- Level 13 onward: 10 × 10 grid, with longer routes and increasing decoy pipes.

## Lives, countdown and scoring

Each run starts with **3 lives shared across all levels**. Every level has a countdown that scales with the board size and level number:

```text
Time limit = min(110, 50 + (board size - 6) × 8 + min(30, level - 1)) seconds
```

A wrong **TEST WATER FLOW** check costs one life but leaves the current board and timer unchanged. If the countdown reaches zero, the run ends immediately; timer expiry does not consume a life first. Three failed flow tests also end the run. Choosing **RESTART FROM LEVEL 1** starts a new run with 3 lives and 0 points.

### Level score

A successful level awards:

- **100 base points** for restoring the water supply.
- **5 points for every second remaining** on the countdown.
- **Up to 100 efficiency points**, reduced by 5 for each pipe turn beyond the puzzle's generated optimal rotation count.
- **20 points deducted per hint used.**

The level score has a minimum of 25 points. The total score accumulates across completed levels in the current run. The countdown continues when the player resets the pipe layout, so Reset cannot be used to gain extra time.



### Life and timer rules

- Rotating pipe pieces never costs a life. The live water preview is informational only.
- Pressing **TEST WATER FLOW** when the school is not connected costs exactly one life. The player can continue editing the same board while time remains.
- A successful test completes the level.
- Three failed tests end the run and offer a restart at Level 1.
- If the countdown reaches zero, the run ends immediately. Timer expiry does not consume a life first.
- The illustrated school, tank reserve, and status label change as the timer falls to show the community impact of unreliable water access.
